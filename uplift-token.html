    Monetary Policy & Safeguards

    Supply

    • Fixed supply; no discretionary minting.
    • Contract renounced/locked where possible.
    • Any changes require public governance and timelock.

    Buyback Mechanics

    • TWAP-based DCA to avoid price manipulation.
    • Max buyback per epoch: ≤ 0.5% of 30d volume or ≤ 10% of B/B wallet, whichever lower.
    • Slippage guard: ≤ 2%; otherwise order splits.

    Burn Policy

    • Monthly consolidated burns with on-chain proofs.
    • Emergency pause switch (multi‑sig) for extreme volatility.
    • Transparent reports posted after each burn.

    Treasury Governance

    • Multi‑sig controlled; proposal‑approved spending only.
    • Timelocked disbursements; public receipts and grant logs.
    • Quarterly audits and budget summaries.
